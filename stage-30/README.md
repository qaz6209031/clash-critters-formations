# Chapter 30 formations

All 80 stages from 22 selected Discord submissions in 24 website cards.
Cards keep 1–4, 5–9 and a separate multiple-of-10 boss in every decade.
Supported Tatari levels: 573–619; all are explicitly stated in their own posts.

Collected read-only through the authenticated Discord website on 2026-10-08.
Antzer’s guide supplies 20 references. Casey’s level-573 post supplies boss
30-10 and credits jacobdumbnut’s guide. Vrondius’s level-619 post supplies
30-25–30-29 and credits Layios. Exhaustive lowest-level comparison was waived.

Exact Tatari forms and positions were visually matched to the supplied planner
catalog, including Trippet T2, Sonarbat T3, Tideon T3, Ospisces T2 and Tikowl T2.
The screenshots usually show the next stage after the reported clear.
Antzer’s opening screenshot shows 30-1 after 29-80; the caption explicitly
extends the formation to 30-1–30-4. His 30-20 and 30-30 captions explicitly
include the following four normal stages, which remain separate website cards.
Vrondius’s screenshot shows the beginning of its captioned range and explicitly
deploys 14/15 Tatari. The board preserves all empty cells.

The website displays the poster and links back to the original Discord message.
Caption credits are recorded separately and do not establish verified authorship.
Original screenshots and raw browser captures remain local ignored evidence.
The public source index contains only relevant captions, poster names, posting
timestamps and permalinks. Earlier chapter formations are unchanged.
