# Chapter 36 formations

All 80 stages, 22 selected Discord submissions, and 24 website cards.
Cards follow 1–4, 5–9, and a separate multiple-of-10 boss in every decade.
Observed or explicitly posted Tatari levels: 786–787.

Collected read-only through the authenticated Discord website on 2026-10-07.
The selection prioritizes one supported formation per requested block; the user
waived exhaustive lowest-level comparison. Each reviewed record retains its
source message ID, permalink, actual displayed stage, level evidence and exact
5×5 board. Caption-reported ranges are preserved even when a screenshot shows
the beginning of a range. Base-art screenshots use explicit caption tier
statements, with tier provenance and confidence retained in the local dataset.
Ambiguous tier exceptions were replaced with clearer sources.

Posting display names are shown on the site and linked to the source message.
Caption credits are recorded separately and do not establish verified authorship.
Original images and browser captures are local ignored evidence. The public
source index includes only captions, poster names, timestamps and permalinks.
Earlier chapters and their grouping were left unchanged.
